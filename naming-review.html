ktp-stripe — PaymentStatus.fromString catches bare Exception where its siblings catch IllegalArgumentException, converting unrelated failures into HTTP 400. The webhook route also logs the handler exception at warn and rethrows, which likely double-logs once Ktor's status handling reports it too.
